## Multiplication — 1

Which expression matches 4 equal groups of 6?

Count the groups first, then the objects in each group.

- A. 4 + 6
  Explicação: That adds the two numbers once; it does not show four equal groups.
- B. 4 × 6
  Explicação: Correct. Four groups with six in each group are 4 × 6.
- C. 6 − 4
  Explicação: Subtraction removes objects instead of making equal groups.
- D. 6 ÷ 4
  Explicação: Division asks about sharing or grouping a total.

Resposta: b

## Multiplication — 2

An array has 3 rows and 8 dots in each row. How many dots are there?

Use rows × dots per row.

- A. 11
  Explicação: That is 3 + 8, but every row contains eight dots.
- B. 21
  Explicação: Check the skip-counting pattern: 8, 16, 24.
- C. 24
  Explicação: Correct. 3 × 8 = 24.
- D. 32
  Explicação: That would be four groups of eight, not three.

Resposta: c

## Multiplication — 3

What is the best way to decompose 7 × 13?

Split 13 into friendly place-value parts.

- A. (7 × 10) + (7 × 3)
  Explicação: Correct. Keep 7 and split 13 into 10 + 3.
- B. 7 × 10 × 3
  Explicação: Multiplying all three numbers changes the value.
- C. (7 + 10) × 3
  Explicação: This changes both the grouping and the operation.
- D. 70 + 13
  Explicação: The second partial product should be 7 × 3, not 13.

Resposta: a

## Multiplication — 4

What is 46 × 10?

Think about each digit becoming ten times as valuable.

- A. 46
  Explicação: Multiplying by one keeps a number the same; ×10 changes place value.

  Explicação: Adding ten is different from multiplying by ten.
- C. 406
  Explicação: The digits must shift together one place to the left.
- D. 460
  Explicação: Correct. Each digit shifts one place to the left.

Resposta: d

## Multiplication — 5

What is 38 × 6 using long multiplication?

Multiply the ones, regroup, and then multiply the tens.

- A. 208
  Explicação: Check the regrouped tens after multiplying 8 × 6.
- B. 228
  Explicação: Correct. 8 × 6 = 48; write 8, regroup 4 tens, then 3 × 6 + 4 = 22.
- C. 380
  Explicação: That resembles 38 × 10, not ×6.
- D. 2,280
  Explicação: The product has one extra place-value position.

Resposta: b

Make as many complete groups of ten as possible.

  Explicação: A remainder must be smaller than the divisor.

## Remainders — 9

95 students travel in vans that hold 10 students each. How many vans are needed?

Every student needs a seat.

- A. 9 vans
  Explicação: Nine vans hold only 90 students, leaving five without seats.
- B. 9.5 vans
  Explicação: A school cannot use half of a van.
- C. 5 vans
  Explicação: That confuses the remainder with the number of groups.
- D. 10 vans
  Explicação: Correct. The remainder means one more complete van is needed.

Resposta: d

## Division — 10

Which sequence shows the long-division steps in the correct order?

Follow the same cycle shown in the material.

- A. Divide, multiply, subtract, bring down
  Explicação: Correct. Repeat this cycle until there are no more digits to bring down.
- B. Add, multiply, bring down, divide
  Explicação: Long division does not begin with addition.
- C. Multiply, add, divide, subtract
  Explicação: The quotient digit must be found before multiplying.
- D. Bring down, ignore the divisor, add
  Explicação: The divisor is needed in every cycle.

Resposta: a

## Mixed operations — 19

A shop packs 6 pencils in each box. How many pencils are in 14 boxes?

Equal groups suggest multiplication.

- A. 20
  Explicação: That adds boxes and pencils per box.
- B. 80
  Explicação: Check by decomposing 14 into 10 + 4.
- C. 96
  Explicação: That product does not match 6 × 14.
- D. 84
  Explicação: Correct. 6 × 10 + 6 × 4 = 60 + 24 = 84.

Resposta: d
