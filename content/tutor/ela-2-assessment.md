## Story elements — 1

Which detail belongs to the setting?

Setting tells when and where.

- A. Maya is patient
  Explicação: That is a personality trait.
- B. The friends solve the mystery
  Explicação: That describes a plot event or solution.
- C. On a rainy Tuesday in the school library
  Explicação: Correct. It gives both time and place.
- D. The missing key
  Explicação: That may be part of the problem.

Resposta: c

## Story elements — 2

What is the plot of a story?

Think about what happens from beginning to end.

- A. Only the place
  Explicação: Place is one part of the setting.
- B. The sequence of important events
  Explicação: Correct. The plot connects the important events.
- C. A list of adjectives
  Explicação: Adjectives can describe details but are not the plot.
- D. Only the title
  Explicação: A title may hint at the story but does not contain its full sequence.

Resposta: b

## Character traits — 3

Lena returns a wallet she found even though nobody saw her. Which trait is best supported?

Use the action as evidence.

- A. Noisy
  Explicação: Returning the wallet does not show how loudly she behaves.
- B. Shy
  Explicação: The action does not prove that she avoids attention.
- C. Careless
  Explicação: She makes a responsible choice, not a careless one.
- D. Honest
  Explicação: Correct. Returning the wallet supports the trait honest.

Resposta: d

## Chronological order — 9

Which sequence is chronological?

Put events in the order they happened.

- A. Finally, first, next
  Explicação: The signal words are out of order.
- B. Tomorrow, yesterday, today
  Explicação: This moves backward and forward in time.
- C. First, next, then, finally
  Explicação: Correct. The sequence moves from beginning to end.
- D. Effect, cause, title, setting
  Explicação: These are not timeline markers.

Resposta: c

## Chronological order — 10

Which step should come first when putting story events in chronological order?

Chronological order follows the events from beginning to end.

- A. Start with the final solution
  Explicação: The solution normally comes after the problem and important actions.
- B. Identify what happened earliest
  Explicação: Correct. Begin with the earliest event, then continue in time order.
- C. Arrange events by sentence length
  Explicação: Sentence length does not show when an event happened.
- D. Choose the funniest event
  Explicação: Preference does not determine chronological order.

Resposta: b

## Summary — 11

Which detail belongs in a summary?

Keep what is necessary to understand the main idea.

- A. The central problem and how it changes
  Explicação: Correct. This is essential to the story's meaning.
- B. The color of every character's socks
  Explicação: That minor detail is usually unnecessary.
- C. Every sentence copied from the text
  Explicação: A summary is shorter and uses your own words.
- D. A new event invented by the reader
  Explicação: A summary must remain faithful to the text.

Resposta: a

## Summary — 12

What should a strong summary avoid?

A summary is concise and accurate.

- A. The main idea
  Explicação: The main idea is essential.
- B. Important events
  Explicação: Key events may be necessary.
- C. Logical order
  Explicação: Logical order makes the summary clear.
- D. Repeated and minor details
  Explicação: Correct. These make a summary longer without improving meaning.

Resposta: d

## Imagery — 13

Which sentence uses sound imagery?

Listen for a detail you could hear.

- A. The lemon tasted sharp
  Explicação: That is taste imagery.
- B. The gate creaked and slammed
  Explicação: Correct. Creaked and slammed appeal to hearing.
- C. The blanket felt rough
  Explicação: That is touch imagery.
- D. The sunset glowed orange
  Explicação: That is sight imagery.

Resposta: b

## Imagery — 14

Which revision creates stronger imagery?

Choose precise sensory language.

- A. The food was nice
  Explicação: Nice is vague and gives no sensory detail.
- B. There was food
  Explicação: This only states that food existed.
- C. Warm cinnamon drifted from the crisp apple pie
  Explicação: Correct. Smell, temperature and texture make the scene vivid.
- D. The food was very, very good
  Explicação: Repeating a vague adjective is less effective than precise detail.

Resposta: c

## Main idea — 15

How is a main idea different from a detail?

Think central message versus support.

- A. The main idea is central; details explain or support it
  Explicação: Correct. Details help develop the central idea.
- B. A detail is always longer
  Explicação: Length does not decide importance.
- C. The main idea must be the first sentence
  Explicação: It may be stated elsewhere or inferred.
- D. There is no difference
  Explicação: They have different roles in comprehension.

Resposta: a

Physical traits show appearance; personality traits show how someone behaves.
