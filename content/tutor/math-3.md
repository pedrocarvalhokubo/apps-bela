## Fractions of a whole

ID: m3-fractions

Numerator, denominator and equal parts

A fraction represents equal parts of a whole. The denominator tells how many equal parts make one whole; the numerator tells how many of those parts are selected.

### Equal parts

The pieces must have the same size. Four unequal pieces do not make fourths.

### Numerator

In 3/4, 3 is the numerator: three parts are selected.

### Denominator

In 3/4, 4 is the denominator: one whole is split into four equal parts.

### Fraction names

1/2 = one half; 1/3 = one third; 1/4 = one fourth; 1/5 = one fifth; 1/6 = one sixth; 1/7 = one seventh; 1/8 = one eighth.

### One whole

All the parts together make one whole: 4/4 = 1 and 6/6 = 1.

Lembre: Denominator = equal parts in ONE whole. Numerator = parts selected.

### Aprofundamento

For the same whole, a larger denominator means smaller pieces. One sixth is smaller than one fourth, because the whole was divided into more pieces.

Português: denominador indica em quantas partes iguais o inteiro foi dividido; numerador conta as partes escolhidas.

- Whole: inteiro
- Numerator: numerador: partes escolhidas
- Denominator: denominador: partes iguais em um inteiro

Exemplo: Divide one circle into 6 equal parts. Select 4: the fraction is 4/6.

Desafio: Draw a whole divided into 8 equal parts and shade 3. Label the numerator and denominator.

## Fraction of a set

ID: m3-sets

Share equally, then choose groups

To find a fraction of a set, divide the total into the number of equal groups shown by the denominator. Then take the number of groups shown by the numerator.

### Divide first

1/5 of 20 marbles: 20 ÷ 5 = 4 marbles.

### Multiply next

3/4 of 56 students: 56 ÷ 4 × 3 = 42 students.

### One group

1/4 of 56 students = 14 students.

### Equal sharing

24 cupcakes shared equally by 6 teachers: 24 ÷ 6 = 4 cupcakes each.

### Do not mix up the total

When finding 1/5 of 20, the denominator is 5. The total number of objects is 20.

Lembre: Total ÷ denominator × numerator. Keep the unit in your answer.

### Aprofundamento

A set is a collection of objects. The denominator tells how many equal groups to form; it is not automatically the number of objects.

Português: primeiro divida pelo número de grupos; depois pegue a quantidade de grupos pedida.

- Set: coleção ou conjunto
- Share equally: dividir em grupos iguais
- Of: de: encontrar a parte da quantidade

Exemplo: 3/4 of 56: four groups of 14; select three groups, so 14 + 14 + 14 = 42.

Desafio: There are 24 counters. Find 1/6 and 5/6 of the set. Show the equal groups.

## Equivalent fractions and mixed numbers

ID: m3-equivalence

Same value, different parts

Equivalent fractions represent the same amount of the same whole. A mixed number combines a whole-number part and a fraction.

### Equivalent fractions

Multiply or divide BOTH numerator and denominator by the same nonzero number: 2/5 = 4/10; 12/24 = 6/12.

### Proper fraction

The numerator is smaller than the denominator: 2/5 is less than one.

### Improper fraction

The numerator is at least the denominator. 7/3 is greater than one; 3/3 is exactly one.

### Mixed to improper

Whole × denominator + numerator gives the new numerator. Keep the denominator: 2 1/3 = 7/3.

### Improper to mixed

Divide numerator by denominator: 7 ÷ 3 = 2 remainder 1, so 7/3 = 2 1/3.

Lembre: Equal value needs the SAME whole. Change numerator and denominator together.

### Aprofundamento

Examples in the school material: 1 2/6 = 8/6; 2 2/3 = 8/3; 2 5/6 = 17/6; 1 3/8 = 11/8. Count all selected parts across the wholes.

Português: número misto tem inteiro e fração. Para transformar, conte quantas partes cabem nos inteiros e acrescente as restantes.

- Equivalent: equivalente: mesmo valor
- Improper fraction: fração imprópria
- Mixed number: número misto

Exemplo: 2 5/6: two wholes contain 12 sixths; add 5 sixths: 17/6.

Desafio: Show that 1/2 = 3/6. Then convert 2 1/3 to an improper fraction.

## Tenths and hundredths

ID: m3-decimals

Fractions, grids and decimal numbers

A decimal point separates the whole-number part from the decimal part. The first place after the point is tenths; the second is hundredths.

### Reading

1.2 = one and two tenths. 71.82 = seventy-one and eighty-two hundredths. You can also read digits after “point”.

### Tenths

7/10 = 0.7; 16/10 = 1 6/10 = 1.6.

### Hundredths

35/100 = 0.35; 5/100 = 0.05. The zero in the tenths place matters.

### More than one

1 6/100 = 1.06; 1 60/100 = 1.60; 2 82/100 = 2.82.

### Back to fractions

1.9 = 1 9/10 = 19/10; 3.04 = 3 4/100; 1.23 = 1 23/100; 0.09 = 9/100.

### Compare and order

Compare wholes, then tenths, then hundredths. 0.89 < 0.90. Increasing order: 0.09, 0.89, 0.9, 1.4, 1.45, 2.65, 4.09.

Lembre: 0.6 = 0.60, but 0.06 is different. One whole grid contains 100 hundredths.

### Aprofundamento

Each square in a 10 × 10 chart is one hundredth; each complete row of ten squares is one tenth. Ten tenths and one hundred hundredths both make one whole.

The material uses both decimal points and decimal commas. In the games, 1.06 and 1,06 are accepted as the same value. Commas in the distance table, such as 1,960 km, separate thousands.

- Tenth: décimo: 1/10
- Hundredth: centésimo: 1/100
- Whole part: parte inteira

Exemplo: Shade 47 of 100 cells: 47/100 = 0.47. Shade a whole grid and 6 more cells: 1.06.

Desafio: Write 0.8 and 0.09 as fractions. Put 1.4, 0.89 and 0.9 in increasing order.

## Adding and subtracting decimals

ID: m3-operations

Line up the decimal points

Write the numbers in columns with decimal points directly under each other. Add or subtract from right to left, regrouping when needed.

### Align

Ones under ones, tenths under tenths, hundredths under hundredths.

### Add zeros

3.2 = 3.20. A final zero helps line up places without changing the value.

### Addition

22.3 + 34.1 = 56.4.

### Subtraction

76.3 − 34.1 = 42.2. For 3.20 − 2.85, regroup: the result is 0.35.

### Keep the point

Write the answer’s decimal point directly under the points above.

Lembre: Line up the POINTS, not the last digits. Check each column.

### Aprofundamento

If a column adds to ten or more, carry to the next place. In subtraction, exchange one unit for ten tenths or one tenth for ten hundredths when needed.

Português: alinhe os separadores, complete com zeros e faça a conta da direita para a esquerda.

- Align: alinhar
- Regroup: reagrupar: vai um ou troca
- Difference: diferença: resultado da subtração

Exemplo: 3.20 − 2.85: exchange one tenth for ten hundredths, then one unit for ten tenths. Result: 0.35.

Desafio: Set out and solve 22.3 + 34.1 and 3.2 − 2.85 in columns.

## Money, distance and temperature

ID: m3-problems

Choose the operation and explain

Read what the question asks before choosing an operation. Keep money, distance and temperature units in the answer.

### Money

Saved i$260; i$5 left means spending i$255. Check how many activities the question asks for.

### School activity prices

Free play 25; movie 200; movie with popcorn 140; digital center 60; all centers 100; free clothing 150; buddy reading 30, all in i$.

### Four activities

Free play + movie with popcorn + digital center + buddy reading: 25 + 140 + 60 + 30 = 255; 260 − 255 = 5.

### Distance difference

Liam ran 3.2 km; Jack ran 2.85 km. “How much farther?” means subtract: 3.20 − 2.85 = 0.35 km.

### Order a table

From Salvador: Belo Horizonte 1,370 km; Brasília 1,460; Rio de Janeiro 1,640; São Paulo 1,960; Curitiba 2,100, in increasing order.

### Temperature rise

29.8°C rising by 2.5°C: 29.8 + 2.5 = 32.3°C.

Lembre: Read → choose an operation → calculate → check the unit and the question.

### Aprofundamento

“Left as change” asks how much remains after spending. “Farther than” compares two distances. “Rise by” tells how much to add.

Português: procure o que está sendo pedido; não escolha a operação só porque viu uma palavra. Confira o sentido da resposta no problema.

- Change: troco
- Farther: mais longe
- Rise by: aumentar em

Exemplo: A budget of 260 with 5 left allows a total cost of 255. Select exactly four activities and check both conditions.

## Perimeter

ID: m3-perimeter

Walk around the outside

Perimeter is the total distance around the outside of a flat shape. Add all the side lengths, using the same unit.

### Rectangle

Opposite sides are equal. With length 5 m and width 3 m: 5 + 3 + 5 + 3 = 16 m.

### Square

Four equal sides. A square with side 25 cm has perimeter 100 cm.

### Regular polygons

All sides are equal. To find one side, divide the perimeter by the number of sides.

### Find a side

Square: 20 ÷ 4 = 5 cm. Regular pentagon: 25 ÷ 5 = 5 cm. Regular hexagon: 60 ÷ 6 = 10 cm.

### Compare units

500 m = 0.5 km. A square with 500 m sides has perimeter 2 km; a triangle with three 1 km sides has 3 km; a 1.2 km × 0.5 km rectangle has 3.4 km.

### Missing information

Do not assume two triangle sides are equal just because they look equal. You need lengths, equality marks or a statement.

Lembre: Perimeter = outside boundary. Use cm, m or km, without a square sign.

### Aprofundamento

Perimeter can also be counted along the exposed edges of square cells. An edge shared by two cells is inside the shape, so do not count it.

Português: contorno é diferente de espaço por dentro. Em polígonos regulares, divida o contorno pelo número de lados para descobrir um lado.

- Perimeter: perímetro: contorno
- Regular: regular: todos os lados iguais
- Length / width: comprimento / largura

Exemplo: A 30 cm × 20 cm rectangle: 30 + 20 + 30 + 20 = 100 cm.

Desafio: Find the perimeter of a 40 cm × 10 cm rectangle. A regular hexagon has perimeter 60 cm: find one side.

## Area and a house on a grid

ID: m3-area

Count square units inside

Area measures the space inside a shape. Count the square units that cover it. For a rectangle or square, multiply length by width.

### Square units

One 1 cm × 1 cm square has area 1 cm². Area units include cm² and m².

### Rectangle

A 9 m × 6 m rectangle has area 9 × 6 = 54 m² and perimeter 30 m.

### Square

A 5 × 5 square covers 25 square units.

### Count cells

A 4 × 3 grid has 12 square units. A 5 × 6 grid has 30. A 3 × 3 grid has 9.

### Draw a house

First bedroom: 8 cm² blue; second bedroom: 6 cm² yellow; bathroom: 4 cm² red; kitchen: 12 cm² green; living room: 16 cm² orange; balcony: 2 cm² pink.

### Area is not perimeter

The same area can have different perimeters. A 2 × 6 rectangle and a 3 × 4 rectangle both cover 12 square units, but their perimeters are 16 and 14 units.

Lembre: Area = inside squares. Perimeter = outside edges. Label each with the right unit.

### Aprofundamento

For an irregular shape made of full squares, count each square once. In the house, rooms must not overlap and the cells of each room should connect along edges.

Português: área conta os quadradinhos por dentro. No retângulo, linhas × colunas dá a quantidade de quadradinhos.

- Area: área: espaço interno
- Square unit: unidade quadrada
- Grid: malha quadriculada

Exemplo: A room 11 m long and 7 m wide has area 77 m² and perimeter 36 m.

Desafio: Draw a room with area 12 square units. Give its length, width, area and perimeter.
